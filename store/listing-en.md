## Full description (max. 4000 characters)
A resistor in your hand, but what was yellow-violet-red again? Resistor Calculator turns color bands into values and values into color bands – fast, clear and fully offline.

⚡ VALUE → COLORS
• Type a value like 4.7, 470 or 2.2 – pick the unit with one tap (Ω, kΩ, MΩ, GΩ)
• Understands schematic notation too: 4k7, 4R7, 2M2
• Instantly shows the matching bands and their color names

🎨 COLORS → VALUE
• Tap the band colors and read the value
• Both modes share the same resistor – switching keeps your value

📐 4, 5 AND 6 BANDS
• Tolerance with minimum and maximum value
• Temperature coefficient (TCR) for 6-band resistors

📏 E-SERIES (IEC 60063)
• E3, E6, E12, E24, E48, E96 and E192
• Suggests the nearest standard value – one tap applies it

📖 REFERENCE
• Full color code table incl. tolerance and TCR
• Reading direction and band layout
• Overview of all E-series

✨ YOUR APP, YOUR STYLE
• 5 designs: Midnight, Light, Cyber, Plasma and AMOLED (saves battery on OLED screens)
• 60 languages
• Tap the result to copy it
• Animations, haptic feedback and fullscreen can be adjusted

🔒 PRIVACY
No ads, no tracking, no account – the app doesn’t even have internet access. Your settings stay on your device.

Great for electronics hobbyists, students, apprentices and the workshop.
